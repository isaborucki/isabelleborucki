04 / About

Connecting questions,
methods & people.

I am Professor for Methodology and Democracy in Digital Transformation at Philipps-University Marburg. My research connects political organizations and comparative politics with digitalization, political communication, and methodological integration.

Academic background ↗

Before joining Marburg, I served as an interim professor at the University of Siegen and led DIPART, the junior research group on digital party research at the University of Duisburg-Essen.

My earlier research and teaching took me to the Universities of Trier and Würzburg. I completed my PhD in political science in 2013 and my habilitation at Duisburg-Essen in 2021.
